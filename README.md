# My Favorite Anime: Hunter x Hunter

> "You should enjoy the little detours to the fullest. Because that's where you'll find the things more important than what you want." – Ging Freecss

## Overview
**Hunter x Hunter** is an action/adventure series that follows *Gon Freecss*, a young boy who discovers that his father is a world-renowned Hunter. Gon embarks on a journey to take the dangerous Hunter Examination and search for his father.

### Main Characters
1. **Gon Freecss** - *Enhancer & Determined Young Hunter*
2. **Killua Zoldyck** - *Transmuter & Former Assassin*
3. **Kurapika** - *Conjurer & Last Survivor of the Kurta Clan*
4. **Leorio Paradinight** - *Emitter & Aspiring Doctor*

### Key Features of the Series
* **Intricate Power System:** The highly tactical and balanced Nen system.
* **Unpredictable Story Arcs:** Shifting from tournament arcs to psychological thrillers and war.
* **Complex Antagonists:** Well-developed villains with deep motivations.

## Why I Like It
* Unmatched strategic battles where intelligence beats raw strength
* The iconic friendship dynamic between Gon and Killua
* Incredible world-building and diverse story arcs
* Exceptional character development for both heroes and villains

## Series Information
* **Genre:** Action / Adventure / Fantasy
* **Release Year:** 2011
* **Studio:** Madhouse
